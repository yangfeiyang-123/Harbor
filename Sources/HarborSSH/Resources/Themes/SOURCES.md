## Anthropic Light and Anthropic Dark (Harbor 0.28.0)

`anthropic.json` is generated by `scripts/build-anthropic-theme.py` from a small set of design tokens: an ivory or warm-charcoal canvas, a slightly darker chrome, raised surfaces, four ink steps and one clay accent. The palettes are Harbor's own design in the spirit of anthropic.com's warm paper and ink; no Anthropic font, logo or other asset is redistributed. The generator checks syntax, text and terminal colors against contrast floors before it writes the file. Headings on native pages use the system serif.

## Tide, Kelp, Dusk, Fog and Lighthouse (Harbor 0.35.0)

`tide.json`, `kelp.json`, `dusk.json`, `fog.json` and `lighthouse.json` are generated by `scripts/build-themes.py` from the same kind of design tokens as the Claude theme, and through the same `palette` and `check` (it loads `scripts/build-anthropic-theme.py` for them), so every file has the roles `anthropic.json` has. The palettes are Harbor's own: Tide is fog-blue paper and deep-sea navy with a sea-blue accent; Kelp is lichen paper and pine black with moss green; Dusk is lilac paper and aubergine with violet; Fog is grays only, with ink for its accent and quiet syntax colors; Lighthouse is white and pitch black at full contrast with one amber accent. None is taken from another editor's or product's theme. Beyond the Claude theme's floors, the generator holds body text to 7:1 on every surface it is set on and status colors to 4.5:1.
